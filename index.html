01 · About

I like AI systems that hold up under scrutiny.

I'm K. Sri Rishikesh Varma, an AI engineer who builds agentic systems in Python: tool-using agents, local LLM runtimes, multi-agent orchestration with MCP and A2A, and the eval and tracing tooling that shows whether they actually work.

I don't trust a number until I know how it was produced. The eval harness I wrote for my own LLM compiler caught four bugs in my code, including a validator that passed everything because it read the wrong JSON path.

Right now I'm building DUM-E, a private AI agent that runs entirely on my own GPU, at my lab DUM-E Labs, and MCP-Shield, a firewall for AI agents, as my final-year project. I'm also exploring speech for Indian languages, starting with Telugu text-to-speech.
